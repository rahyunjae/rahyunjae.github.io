About Me

I'm an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University, advised by Prof. Sungeun Hong. My research focuses on efficient model learning, model merging, and multimodal learning.

I'm interested in making large multimodal models more efficient to learn and adapt, combining independently trained models, and improving the reliability of multimodal reasoning. My goal is to make powerful AI more practical and accessible under limited compute. Always happy to chat about research and potential collaborations.
