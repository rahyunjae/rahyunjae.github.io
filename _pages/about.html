About Me

I'm an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University, advised by Prof. Sungeun Hong. My research spans efficient model learning, model merging, multimodal learning, hallucination mitigation, and trustworthy multimodal AI.

I work on making large multimodal models efficient to adapt and reliable in their reasoning, with the goal of making capable AI practical under limited compute. I'm always happy to discuss research and collaborations.
